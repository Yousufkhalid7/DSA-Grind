75. Sort Colors
Approach: In-place swapping

Solution:
class Solution(object):
    def sortColors(self, nums):
        i = 1 
        while i != len(nums):
            if nums[i] < nums[i-1]:
                nums[i], nums[i-1] = nums[i-1], nums[i]
                i = 1
            else:
                i += 1
